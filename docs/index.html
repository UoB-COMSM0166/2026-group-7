✿ About the Game

Park Street Survivor is a browser-based narrative runner set in Bristol. You play as Iris, an MSc student navigating five days of academic pressure, social encounters, and the very real obstacles of Park Street hill — all while managing her mental health and making it through the week.

Each day starts in Iris's room: make choices, pack your bag, prepare for what's ahead. Then run the gauntlet — dodge scooters, puddles, and commuters on the slope — and see if you can hold it together. The game explores themes of burnout and student anxiety through five interconnected levels with branching dialogue and item-based strategy.

✿ Core Design Pillars
